# Brief — Petpooja Marketing Automation: Meta Ads with AI

- **Goal:** show a restaurant owner how to create and launch a Facebook/Instagram ad with AI in one flow.
- **Audience:** restaurant owners & managers in India; comfortable with Hinglish, not with ad tools.
- **Promise:** "Na designer, na agency — ek prompt se poora ad."
- **CTA:** Try Petpooja Marketing Automation today + subscribe.
- **Format:** YouTube, 1920×1080, 30 fps, Hinglish VO (female), target 3:00–3:30.
- **Logo:** `brand/petpooja-marketing-automation.svg` (brand red `#C52031`; white wordmark on dark).
- **Recording:** `source/raw/` — HQ take in 2 parts (3420×2146 @60 fps, no audio): `01 First Draft.mov` (0:45,
  onboarding → identity → loading) + `02 Second draft.mov` (3:14, dashboard → prompt → AI → details → review).
  Working copy `source/screen.mp4` = both parts joined, browser chrome cropped (3276:2050:112:96), 2560 px, 30 fps.
- **This take's picks:** Aesthetic / Loud / Retro / Let AI decide · Anton + Barlow · "Re-engage lapsed customers"
  → 10% OFF valid this month · Style 2 · ad "We miss you · Tuna Pizza" · Call Now · 2 locations · ₹15,000, 1 week.
- **Do not show:** the payment popup (UPI QR) after Pay & Launch — end the screen footage on the click.

## Flow (8 steps → YouTube chapters)
1. Open Meta Platforms (Advertisements → Meta Platforms)
2. Choose creative styles (onboarding: offer / new launch / festival / everything else)
3. Brand identity (logo, colours, fonts) → Let's Create Ad → AI learns the restaurant
4. Describe the campaign (quick idea chip → AI prompt → edit the offer → submit)
5. Let AI build the ad (answer questions → pick a style → Go ahead → creative + copy)
6. Edit copy, CTA & location
7. Budget & schedule
8. Review, pay & launch
